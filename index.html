Platform Pitch

A property platform that helps your business grow, not one that just lists homes.

A full website, agent tools, and lead management, all live, mobile ready, and built around your team.

The Problem

Most agencies are still running on WhatsApp and Instagram alone.

  • No central record
    Leads end up in someone's phone instead of in the business.
  • Buyers can't help themselves
    Every enquiry needs a person to reply before anyone can properly look at a listing.
  • Google can't see you
    Social posts don't get indexed, so search traffic goes to competitors who have real websites.
The Platform

Everything your business needs, in one place.

Public website

A branded homepage, searchable listings, agent profiles, and property pages that show up on Google.

Admin dashboard

Add listings, manage photos, and keep track of your leads. No developer needed for everyday use.

Agent accounts

Every agent gets their own login, profile, and lead inbox, and you review each one before it goes live.

Buyer accounts

Visitors can save their favorites and reach an agent straight away by WhatsApp or enquiry form.

For Buyers

Make it easy to go from browsing to talking with an agent.

  • Search and filter by location, price, and property type.
  • Save favorites and come back to them anytime, from any device.
  • One tap WhatsApp straight to the listing agent, with the property already mentioned.
  • Chat right on the site with the agent, so nobody has to leave the platform to ask a question.
For Agents

Every agent gets their own storefront and pipeline.

  • List and edit properties with photo galleries, from a phone or a laptop.
  • A personal profile page showing their listings, contact details, and experience.
  • A real lead inbox where every enquiry lands with the right agent automatically.
  • Live chat with buyers so agents can reply in real time from the same dashboard.
For the Business

Control and visibility, without adding headcount.

  • Vet every agent because new agent accounts stay inactive until an admin approves them.
  • See everything across the company with every listing and lead from every agent in one dashboard.
  • SEO that comes built in so every listing is set up for Google, with a sitemap that updates itself.
Where This Takes You

A platform that keeps getting better as you grow.

More listings bring more search visibility. More agents mean more coverage. And every enquiry gets captured instead of getting lost in a DM.

Next up: alerts for saved searches, agent ratings, and a custom domain, all built on the same foundation and ready when you are.
